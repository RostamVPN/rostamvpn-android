## Full description
<!-- max 4000 -->
```
Your digital guardian.

RostamVPN has been installed more than half a million times. It is built and run by network and security engineers who have spent over a decade keeping the internet open.

RostamVPN is a fast, private VPN for Android. Install it, press connect, and you are on the open internet. There is no account to create, no email to give, and nothing to set up.

ONE TAP
Press connect. The app finds a way out for whatever network you are on and keeps it. If the connection drops it recovers by itself. You do not have to choose anything or understand anything.

MADE FOR DIFFICULT NETWORKS
Some networks block, filter or slow things down. RostamVPN is built for those. If one way out stops working, it looks for another one on its own, without asking you.

FREE TO START, AND IT STAYS FREE
Every install comes with an allowance of data at full speed, and that allowance renews every month.

When it runs out you are not disconnected and not locked out. The connection keeps working at a lower speed for as long as you want it. Premium has no cap and never slows down.

In some regions the app is free entirely, paid for by donations. There is no payment screen at all, nothing is counted, and every region is open. The app works this out by itself. You never have to ask for it or prove anything.

NOTHING TO HAND OVER
No account. No email address. No phone number. No name. We never ask for any of them, so there is nothing of that kind for us to know. Removing the app removes everything.

PREMIUM
Premium has no data cap at all, opens every region, and removes the ad shown when you connect. Subscriptions are billed through Google Play and can be cancelled at any time in the Play Store. Prices are shown in your own currency inside the app. Premium is not offered in the regions where the app is already free.

ADS
Free users see one full-screen ad when they connect. That is the only ad in the app, and nothing is ever added to the pages you visit. Premium removes it.

WHAT IS IN THE APP
- One-tap connect, with the region chosen for you
- Or pick your own region, and change it as often as you like
- Choose which apps go through the VPN and which do not
- Keep specific sites outside the VPN, such as your bank
- Usage figures, so you always know what you have used
- Light and dark themes
- Restore Premium on a new phone with an account code, with no sign-in

ABOUT THE VPN CONNECTION
RostamVPN uses Android's VPN service to send your device's traffic through an encrypted connection to our servers, so the network you are on cannot read it or filter it. Android asks your permission the first time you connect, and the connection runs only while you have it switched on. Switching it off returns your traffic to the normal network immediately.

There is also an optional setting that lets your device help other people find a working connection. It is off unless you switch it on, and you can switch it off again whenever you like.

WHO WE ARE
RostamVPN is built and operated in the United States by an international team of network and security engineers and longtime advocates for internet freedom. For over a decade we have worked to strengthen digital security and protect open, uncensored access to information.
```
